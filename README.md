Project Overview

Akatsuki is a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace initiative developed by TCprojects.

The platform is designed to help independent businesses, small retailers, home-based entrepreneurs and online sellers establish a digital presence and reach customers through one central marketplace.

Rather than requiring every business to build and maintain its own e-commerce website, Akatsuki aims to provide a shared environment where approved sellers can showcase their products, manage their storefronts and fulfil customer orders.

Customers will be able to discover products from multiple independent sellers through one shopping experience.

Akatsuki is being developed with an initial focus on the South African market, with a broader vision of creating an accessible digital commerce ecosystem for independent businesses.

Vision

To make online selling more accessible by giving independent businesses the opportunity to participate in digital commerce through one connected marketplace.

The Problem

Many small businesses have products worth selling but lack the resources, technical knowledge or infrastructure to establish their own online stores.

Some depend on social media, messaging applications or physical retail alone to reach customers.

Akatsuki aims to reduce these barriers by providing a shared marketplace where independent sellers can establish digital storefronts and access a wider potential customer base.

The Solution

Akatsuki brings together:

- Independent sellers and businesses.
- Individual digital storefronts.
- Product discovery and shopping.
- Customer accounts and order management.
- Seller product and inventory management.
- Marketplace administration.
- A planned integrated payment and seller settlement model.

The objective is to create one connected marketplace for a wide range of products rather than limiting the platform to a single retail category.

Financial Model

Akatsuki's agreed marketplace commission model is based on a 10% commission on product value only.

Shipping charges are excluded from the commission calculation.

Example:

Description| Amount
Product price| R500
Shipping| R60
Customer total| R560
Marketplace commission| R50
Seller proceeds| R510

The financial dashboards are designed to distinguish marketplace revenue from seller proceeds and shipping allocations.

Live payment processing and seller payouts are not yet confirmed as operational.
